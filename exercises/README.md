# Exercises

One log per exercise, numbered: `NNN-<slug>.md`. Each records: what was built, the assumption(s) it encodes, sources surveyed (links), where and why you diverged, and any failures observed since last time — traced to the assumption IDs they falsify.

Exercise 000 is the only pre-written exercise: the assumptions interview that seeds `assumptions.md`. Everything after it comes from your own frictions and failures.
